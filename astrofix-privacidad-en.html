1. Data controller

ASTROFIX is an app developed by RDRP Studio (Rafael Del Rosal, Spanish Tax ID/NIF B13897004), based in Spain. For any question about this policy or about your data, you can write to delrosalr@hotmail.com.

2. General principle: everything happens on your device

ASTROFIX is designed to work entirely offline. All astronomical navigation calculations, the nautical almanac, your observation log, and your settings are processed and stored only on your phone or tablet. The app has no servers of its own, does not require creating a user account, and does not send your data to RDRP Studio or to any third party.

3. Permissions requested by the app

ASTROFIX requests the following system permissions, only for the features that need them:

  • Location (GPS) — used to know your position and, if you enable it, your course and speed, in order to calculate the automatic running fix and the magnetic declination for your area. Your position is used in the calculation itself, on the device, and is not transmitted or stored on any server.
  • Camera — used only for the full-screen camera star finder: it overlays a reticle on the live camera image to help you locate a celestial body. The app does not record or save any photos or video from the camera.
  • Microphone — used only for the experimental voice time-capture feature, which lets you fix the time of a sight by saying "TOP" without letting go of the sextant. The audio is analyzed on the device itself to detect that word and is not recorded or sent anywhere. This feature is optional and can be turned off in Settings.

4. Data stored, and where

ASTROFIX stores, only in your own device's storage: your settings (language, camera corrections, leeway coefficient, etc.), the observation log you choose to keep (position, sights, and configuration for each fix), and, if you export them, the PDF reports you generate. None of this leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself, for example by emailing a PDF.

5. No ads, no analytics, no third parties

ASTROFIX contains no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not integrate any third-party service that receives data from the app. No information is shared with advertisers, social networks, or data brokers.

6. Your rights

Since all of the app's data stays on your device, you have full control over it: you can delete the observation log or reset the settings from within the app itself, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. If you have any questions about how data is handled, you can contact us at the address above.

7. Children

ASTROFIX is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors, consistent with the fact that it does not collect personal data from any user.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this same page will be updated with a new "last updated" date shown at the top. We recommend checking it periodically, especially before updating the app.
